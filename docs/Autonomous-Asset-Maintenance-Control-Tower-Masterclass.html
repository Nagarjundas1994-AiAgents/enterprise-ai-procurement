Chapter 08 — Relationships, Associations and Compositions

What are we learning?

Simple: association = points at someone (supplier); composition = owns children (order owns its lines). Enterprise: compositions cascade-delete and enable deep insert; associations stay independent. Example: delete an order → its assignments go too (composition); delete an order → the technician survives (association).

Why is this important?

Wrong choice deletes master data (catastrophic) or orphans children (audit nightmare).

Where does this fit in the architecture?

Decides OData $expand shapes (Chapter 12) and cascade behavior in transactions (Chapter 14).
